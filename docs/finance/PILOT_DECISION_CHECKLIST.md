# Stage 2 — Pilot decision checklist

Use this checklist for each proposed pilot site and country. Mark each item `PASS`, `OPEN`, or `FAIL` with evidence, owner and review date. A financial scenario cannot override a failed safety, permission or destination gate.

## Mandatory go/no-go gates
- [ ] **Site permission:** written permission or other adequate documented authorization for the planned activities, storage and access.
- [ ] **Local requirements:** Botswana- or Eswatini-specific checks completed with relevant authorities or a qualified local adviser for the chosen activity, materials, transport, storage, business/tax position and worker safety.
- [ ] **Safe scope:** accepted material list excludes hazardous, clinical, chemical and specialist waste unless qualified partners, safeguards and approvals are established.
- [ ] **Recovery destination:** destination/facility identity, acceptance criteria, current ability to receive each material, handover process and required evidence verified.
- [ ] **Worker and participant safety:** risk assessment, training, suitable PPE, safe lifting/handling, incident response, hygiene and supervision arrangements confirmed and costed.
- [ ] **Transport and storage:** suitable transport, safe and permitted temporary storage, weather/fire/security controls, collection frequency and rejected-load contingency confirmed.
- [ ] **Traceability:** IDs, weighing method, transfer evidence, reconciliation responsibilities, discrepancy process and secure record storage are ready.
- [ ] **Accountability:** named roles (not necessarily public personal details) assigned for operations, finance, safety, evidence review and stop-work decisions.

**Any failed mandatory gate means PAUSE.** An open mandatory gate means do not begin collection; resolve it first.

## Commercial and financial gates
- [ ] Customer demand: documented conversations or written expressions of interest from identifiable prospective customer organizations; no demand inferred from social interest alone.
- [ ] Service scope: written scope, collection schedule, service boundaries, responsibilities and proposed fee approved for testing.
- [ ] Cost evidence: dated transport, labour, PPE/consumables, storage, disposal/rejection and partner-fee quotes or observed costs are recorded.
- [ ] Revenue evidence: distinguish proposed fees, executed service agreements, confirmed sponsorship/grants, buyer quotes, actual invoices and cash received.
- [ ] Working capital: funds or committed funding cover setup and the conservative gross working-capital requirement before receipts; do not rely on unpaid invoices.
- [ ] Downside test: scenario still has an acceptable loss under low volume, high transport/labour, contamination/rejection, lower buyer price, payment delay and zero material-sale revenue.
- [ ] Maximum tolerable loss: owner defines a cash loss cap and stop trigger before spending; do not proceed if exposure exceeds the cap.
- [ ] Unit economics: cost per collection, kg collected and kg accepted are understood; break-even outputs have positive contribution and are not based on fabricated inputs.
- [ ] Independent review: another person reviews assumptions, costs, safety, destination evidence and decision where practical; if unavailable, record that limitation.

## Decision rule
- **PROCEED TO A CONTROLLED PILOT:** all mandatory gates pass; customer need is evidenced; safe destination and responsibilities are verified; major costs are quoted/observed; credible fee/funding and working capital exist; and the downside loss is within the agreed cap. Start small, set a review date and stop triggers.
- **REDESIGN:** mandatory gates can be met but economics are weak or uncertain. Narrow material types, reduce collection frequency, use a partner's existing logistics, negotiate a paid service trial, or change the scope; recalculate before committing.
- **PAUSE:** any mandatory gate fails or remains open; no safe verified destination; permissions/compliance are unresolved; worker safety cannot be assured; funding/working capital is absent; maximum loss is undefined/exceeded; or the only viable case depends on speculative material prices or unsigned income.
