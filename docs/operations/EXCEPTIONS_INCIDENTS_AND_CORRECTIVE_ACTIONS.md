# Exceptions, Incidents and Corrective Actions

## Severity
- **CRITICAL:** injury, fire, spill, suspected hazardous exposure, absent permission during work, unsafe transport, serious fraud/data breach, or uncontrolled custody. Stop affected operations; protect people; contact emergency/competent services where appropriate; notify pilot lead immediately.
- **HIGH:** major quantity discrepancy, destination refusal without safe return route, missing load, suspected theft, repeated measurement failure or record tampering. Hold affected batch/transaction and notify the pilot lead promptly.
- **MEDIUM:** missing receipt, incomplete fields, isolated measurement variance, minor contamination or late reconciliation. Assign owner and deadline; keep outcome unverified.
- **LOW:** administrative issue with no material or financial effect.

When uncertain, use the higher severity until assessed.

## Exception lifecycle
1. **REPORTED:** unique exception ID, event time, reporter, affected country/site/collection/batch and factual description.
2. **CONTAINED:** immediate action, stop status, material/location status and notifications.
3. **INVESTIGATING:** assign owner, preserve original records/evidence, distinguish known from unknown.
4. **CORRECTIVE_ACTION_OPEN:** action, owner, due date and prevention measure.
5. **RESOLVED_PENDING_REVIEW:** evidence of correction and resolution time.
6. **CLOSED:** closure criteria met and approved by someone other than the action owner where practicable. Critical/high incidents require pilot lead review and any required competent clearance before restart.
7. **REOPENED:** new evidence contradicts closure; preserve previous history.

Never overwrite an original record to conceal an error. Append a correction/new event with reference, reason, editor, time and reviewer.

## Response matrix

| Type | Immediate containment | Closure evidence | Stop? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer unavailable/access denied | Do not enter or collect; reschedule or mark failed. | Visit outcome and next step. | Stop affected visit. |
| Missing permission | Stop site activity. | Permission verified before restart. | Yes. |
| Unsafe/prohibited material | Keep people away; do not touch/sort; seek competent advice. | Safe route and qualified approval where needed. | Yes for affected work. |
| Scale malfunction | Mark quantity unverified; hold affected claim. | Suitable reweigh or retained estimate label. | Stop verified-weight claim. |
| Missing/duplicate record | Preserve source; prevent duplicate use. | Unique ID and reviewed correction. | Stop affected reconciliation. |
| Contaminated/rejected load | Secure safely; confirm authorized route. | Quantity, destination and evidence. | Stop if route unsafe. |
| Missing receipt/partner refusal | Keep unresolved; contact destination. | Dated evidence or explicitly unresolved status. | Stop acceptance/recycling claim. |
| Stock difference/theft/damage | Secure stock if safe; recount with second person. | Movement review and approved resolution. | Stop dispatch if custody uncertain. |
| Transport failure | Secure load and people; do not abandon material. | Safe onward plan and custody record. | Yes until safe. |
| Injury/exposure/spill/fire | Stop; follow emergency response and competent advice. | Incident report and required clearance/corrective action. | Yes. |
| Missing funds/disputed payment | Preserve transaction evidence; suspend unsupported posting. | Reconciled payment or recorded dispute. | Stop disputed closure. |
| Suspected fraud/alteration | Restrict access; preserve originals/logs. | Independent review and remediation. | Stop affected workflow. |
| Data exposure/unauthorized access | Limit access and preserve incident evidence. | Containment, access review and applicable notification assessment. | As needed. |

## Escalation and restart
The pilot lead owns escalation and stop-work decisions. Do not delay emergency response for internal approval. Restart only after danger is controlled, permissions/safety/destination conditions are reverified, corrective actions are recorded, and the pilot lead documents approval. Critical incidents require independent review where available.

Use the exception CSV. Keep descriptions factual and do not place sensitive personal/health data in repository records. Store protected evidence separately and use a restricted reference.
