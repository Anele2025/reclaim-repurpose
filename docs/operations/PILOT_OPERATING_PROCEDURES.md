# Stage 3 — Pilot Operating Procedures

**Status:** Draft procedures for controlled pilot preparation. This does not establish that any site, destination, permit, customer, or operation is approved.

## Purpose and scope
Provide a repeatable process from collection planning through destination handover and monthly reconciliation in Botswana (BW) or Eswatini (SZ). Apply only to the country, site, materials and activities whose requirements have been checked. Start with non-hazardous, separately identifiable materials. Do not accept clinical, chemical, hazardous, electronic, battery, pressurised or unknown waste unless a competent specialist process and required approvals are established.

## Roles
- **Pilot lead:** approves scope, verifies site permission and authorizes stop/restart decisions.
- **Collection operator:** screens, weighs, labels and records material safely.
- **Custody/transport responsible person:** protects batch identity and records transfers.
- **Destination representative:** confirms offered, accepted and rejected quantities where evidence is available.
- **Records steward:** checks IDs, completeness, duplicates and reconciliation.
- **Finance recorder:** records incurred costs, invoices and actual receipts separately.
- **Independent reviewer:** reviews serious exceptions, corrections, monthly reconciliation and claims where practicable.

One person may hold multiple roles, but should not be the sole approver of their own discrepancy closure or unsupported impact claim. If no independent reviewer is available, record the limitation and use a second-person check for critical quantities and financial evidence where possible.

## End-to-end workflow

| Step | Trigger / owner | Actions and record | Evidence and validation | Exception path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Approve scope | Before planning / pilot lead | Define country, site, material types, schedule, roles and loss cap; review Stage 2 decision checklist. | Written scope; all mandatory gates passed. | Pause if any gate is open. |
| 2. Verify site | Before first visit / pilot lead | Check identity, access, permission, activity and storage. Update customer/site and compliance records. | Dated permission evidence. | No collection until resolved. |
| 3. Verify destination | Before collecting a stream / pilot lead | Confirm facility identity, acceptance criteria, capacity, packaging, fees and evidence offered. | Dated source and current verification. | Do not collect that stream without a safe verified route. |
| 4. Schedule | Before visit / operator | Confirm time, material, transport and capacity; create collection ID. | Unique ID and confirmation record. | Mark cancelled/failed honestly. |
| 5. Screen and collect | On arrival / operator | Confirm access; screen safely; reject out-of-scope items; label batch IDs. | Site, time, material, responsible role and screening outcome. | Stop if unsafe, prohibited or uncertain. |
| 6. Weigh | At collection / operator | Record scale, gross, tare, net, unit, method and evidence; obtain acknowledgement where practical. | Net = gross − tare; no double tare subtraction. | If unsuitable scale, mark estimate/unverified. |
| 7. Sort and store | At approved location / operator | Record input/output batches, contamination and location in material events. | Batch lineage and measured quantities. | Open exception for discrepancy or unsafe item. |
| 8. Transfer | Before and during transport / custodian | Reconfirm destination; record dispatch, batch, quantity, releasing and receiving roles. | Transfer event and evidence reference. | Stop if destination or transport conditions change. |
| 9. Handover | At destination / operator + receiver | Record offered, accepted, rejected and unresolved quantities separately. | Same units; accepted + rejected must not exceed offered without explanation. | Open exception and assign follow-up. |
| 10. Downstream outcome | After handover / records steward | Record reuse/recycling only with outcome-specific evidence. | Acceptance alone is not recycling evidence. | Keep unverified outcomes unverified. |
| 11. Reconcile | Daily/monthly / records steward + reviewer | Compare collections, movements, handovers, stock, exceptions, costs and receipts. | Material balance and evidence checks documented. | Investigate; never force a balance. |
| 12. Report | Monthly / pilot lead | Report each material stage separately with limitations. | Reviewed report, period, methods and evidence. | Exclude unsupported claims. |

## Evidence hierarchy
Prefer independently verifiable processing records, destination tickets or receipts tied to a batch, then signed handover documents, then contemporaneous logs/photos as supporting evidence. Verbal statements and estimates remain unverified. Evidence is claim-specific: a receipt may support a handover or sale but does not automatically prove recycling. Keep private agreements and personal data in controlled storage.

## Stop and restart
Stop the affected activity for unsafe material, injury, spill, absent permission, unsafe transport/storage, destination refusal, suspected fraud/data breach, or custody discrepancy. Protect people first; isolate the load only if safe; notify the pilot lead and competent/emergency services where appropriate. Restart only after the cause is contained, permissions and safety/destination conditions are reverified, corrective actions are recorded, and the pilot lead documents approval. Financial targets never override a safety stop.

## Manual/offline fallback
Use pre-numbered paper forms or a secure offline note containing operational IDs, event time, material, quantities/units, measurement method, roles, destination and acknowledgement where practical. Mark pending entry, secure the source, enter as soon as practical, and have a second person compare the entry. Preserve event time separately from entry time; never reuse IDs or silently backdate.

## Country checks
For BW and SZ separately, verify local requirements for collection, storage, transport, waste types, worker/volunteer arrangements, business/tax status, permissions, insurance and environmental obligations as applicable. Consult the relevant authority or qualified local adviser. This document is not legal advice.
