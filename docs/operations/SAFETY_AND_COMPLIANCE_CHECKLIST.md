# Safety and Compliance Checklist

**Use separately for each country, site, activity and material stream. This is not legal advice and does not authorize operations.**

## Pre-pilot gate
- [ ] Site identity, access and permission documented for collection, sorting and storage.
- [ ] Country-specific compliance register completed with relevant authority or qualified local adviser; mandatory open items resolved.
- [ ] Destination identity and acceptance conditions verified for every planned material stream.
- [ ] Scope excludes unknown, hazardous, chemical, clinical, battery, electronic, pressurised and specialist waste unless a competent process, safeguards and required approvals exist.
- [ ] Risk assessment covers lifting, cuts, glass, contamination, transport, fire, weather, stacking, manual handling and public access as applicable.
- [ ] Suitable PPE, hygiene supplies and emergency arrangements are available and costed.
- [ ] Operators know stop-work rules, emergency contacts, first aid and incident reporting.
- [ ] Safe transport, temporary storage and rejected-load route are confirmed.
- [ ] Scale and measurement procedure are suitable; estimates can be clearly labelled.
- [ ] Roles, supervision, financial limits and escalation are documented.
- [ ] Records have appropriate access restrictions; no real personal/confidential data is stored in the public repository.
- [ ] Working capital and maximum tolerable loss are documented in the Stage 2 decision record.

Any failed or unresolved mandatory item means **PAUSE**. Financial forecasts cannot override safety, permission or destination gates.

## Daily pre-start
- [ ] Permission/access remains valid.
- [ ] Destination remains able to receive the planned material.
- [ ] Weather, route, transport and storage are suitable.
- [ ] PPE, hygiene, first aid and emergency contact method are available.
- [ ] Scale, containers, labels and recording method are ready.
- [ ] No unknown/prohibited material is planned for handling.
- [ ] Operators understand tasks, safe handling, stop conditions and custody roles.
- [ ] Prior critical/high exceptions are cleared before affected work resumes.

## During and after work
- [ ] Stop if unsafe material, injury, spill, access issue, unsafe load or destination change occurs.
- [ ] Keep people away from unknown/hazardous material; do not open, mix, burn or dispose informally.
- [ ] Record material and custody movements contemporaneously.
- [ ] Secure loads and storage; maintain safe stacking/separation.
- [ ] Clean up safely and wash hands after handling.
- [ ] Record incidents and near misses.
- [ ] Reconcile batches, stock, handovers and discrepancies before closing the day.

## Botswana (BW)
For the actual location/activity, consult the relevant Botswana authority and/or qualified local adviser. Verify which requirements apply to collection, materials, site permission, storage, transport, environmental management, business/tax status, workers/volunteers, insurance and special waste. Record source, URL/reference, date, answer, evidence, permit reference, reviewer and review/expiry date in the compliance register. Do not mark VERIFIED without documentary evidence for the specific requirement.

## Eswatini (SZ)
Perform a separate review with the relevant Eswatini authority and/or qualified local adviser. Do not copy Botswana conclusions or approvals. Record jurisdiction-specific sources, dates, evidence, approvals, open questions and review dates in the compliance register.

## Status definitions
- **NOT_CHECKED:** no meaningful check yet.
- **IN_PROGRESS:** check underway; not permission to operate.
- **VERIFIED:** source and evidence support the specific requirement.
- **NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE:** evidence supports non-applicability.
- **BLOCKED:** unresolved issue prevents safe/legal progress.
- **EXPIRED:** approval/evidence is out of date or no longer valid.

A blank field, assumption or verbal statement alone does not establish verification. Keep confidential documents securely outside the repository and store only a restricted evidence reference.
