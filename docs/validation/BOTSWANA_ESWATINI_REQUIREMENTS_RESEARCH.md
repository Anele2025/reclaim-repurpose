# Botswana and Eswatini — Requirements Research

**Initial research checked:** 2026-10-15. This is a preliminary research note, not legal advice or a determination that a particular pilot activity is permitted. Verify current law, commencement, amendments, regulations, local rules and applicability directly before operations.

## How to use this document
Research Botswana and Eswatini independently. Add one row per question to [country_requirements_register.csv](../../templates/validation/country_requirements_register.csv). For every question, record activity/material, official authority, source URL, date checked, finding, status, action owner and review date. A general statute does not answer every site-specific licensing question.

## Botswana

- Date checked: 2026-10-15.

### Questions to resolve
- Does proposed collection, sorting, temporary storage, transfer or transport require a licence, registration, approval or prescribed plan?
- Are there specific requirements for chosen material categories, including contaminated or special waste?
- Which local authority controls the proposed site, collection route, storage area or waste facility?
- Are there local rules, site conditions, service contracts or approved recovery arrangements that affect the activity?
- What transport, worker safety, insurance, business registration, tax and recordkeeping obligations apply to the actual operating structure?
- Is the receiving facility authorized and willing to accept the exact material and batch?
- What evidence must be retained for movement, handover, rejection and final destination?

The statute's existence is a research lead, not a conclusion about which permissions the proposed pilot requires. Confirm the relevant authority and applicable provisions for the actual activity and site.

## Eswatini

### Initial sources
- **Eswatini Environment Authority — Environmental Law:** https://eea.org.sz/environmental-law/
- **Eswatini Environment Authority — Legislation:** https://eea.org.sz/legislation/
- **EswatiniLII — Environment Management Act, 2002 (Act 5 of 2002):** https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2002/5/eng%402002-11-22
- Date checked: 2026-10-15.

The Environment Management Act, 2002 and EEA's legislation page identify a waste-management framework, including topics such as waste licences and special-waste transport. This note does not determine whether a specific licence applies to a small pilot. Confirm the current consolidated text, regulations and interpretation with the Eswatini Environment Authority or a qualified local adviser.

### Questions to resolve
- Does the proposed activity constitute regulated waste collection, transport, sorting, recovery, storage, disposal or management?
- Is any waste-management, special-waste management or special-waste carrier licence relevant to the proposed materials and activity?
- Does sorting/recovery at a disposal facility require facility-specific authorization?
- What rules apply to the proposed site, local authority, transport route, worker safety and storage arrangement?
- Are environmental assessment, business, tax, insurance or recordkeeping requirements triggered by the actual scope?
- What evidence must the receiving facility provide for acceptance, rejection and transfer?

## Common verification procedure
For each open question, contact the relevant authority using an official channel. State the country, site, exact material, expected activity, storage/transport plan and proposed scale. Ask for applicable law/regulation, licence or approval name, responsible office, application procedure and any exemptions in writing. Save the response in restricted storage and reference it in the register.

Do not mark a requirement CONFIRMED merely because a general law or web page exists. Use UNRESOLVED until applicability to the actual scope is established. Do not transport waste between Botswana and Eswatini without separately verifying all applicable domestic and transboundary requirements.

## Other readiness questions
Check site access/ownership, local council and institutional approval, occupational health and safety, manual handling, PPE, transport/vehicle requirements, fire/weather/security controls, business registration, tax, insurance/liability, consumer or contract issues, and privacy obligations. Assign each question an owner and review date. If a mandatory question remains unresolved, the decision is PAUSE.
