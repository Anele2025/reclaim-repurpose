# Recovery-Partner Qualification Guide

## Purpose
Verify a receiver's ability and willingness to accept a precisely described material stream. An organization's existence, website or general recycling activity is not evidence that it accepts RECLAIM & REPURPOSE's specific materials or quantities.

## Qualification procedure
1. Identify the organization through a public source or consented referral. Record the date and source.
2. Contact it using an official public or consented channel.
3. Ask whether the facility currently accepts the specific material, grade, form, cleanliness and packaging proposed.
4. Confirm contamination limits, moisture requirements, prohibited inclusions and reasons for rejection.
5. Ask for minimum/maximum batch size, operating hours, booking process, delivery/collection responsibilities and location.
6. Request current price basis and currency, weighing method, quotation date, validity/expiry, deductions, fees and payment terms. If there is no price or the receiver charges a gate fee, record that accurately.
7. Confirm who pays for transport and rejected loads, and what happens if material is refused.
8. Ask what receipt, weighbridge ticket, transfer note, invoice or other handover evidence can be provided.
9. Verify whether any permit, licence, site access or documentation is required for the proposed transfer.
10. Preserve a dated, non-sensitive evidence reference. Store confidential correspondence outside the public repository.
11. Reconfirm terms immediately before any proposed physical handover; acceptance and prices can change.
12. Update the Stage 2 model only with dated evidence and maintain an audit trail.

## Evidence hierarchy
Prefer a current written specification, quotation, email confirmation, or written response attributable to the receiving organization. A phone conversation can be logged as a self-reported statement but should be corroborated where practical. Record role, date, method and limitations; do not publish private contact details.

## Qualification status
Use clear contact statuses such as NOT_CONTACTED, CONTACTED, AWAITING_RESPONSE, TERMS_RECEIVED, CONDITIONS_VERIFIED, NOT_ACCEPTING, or UNRESOLVED. The partner register's CommercialStatus should distinguish UNKNOWN, NO_QUOTE, QUOTE_RECEIVED, CONDITIONAL, AGREEMENT_DOCUMENTED, and NO_AGREEMENT.

Do not mark a partner operationally qualified until intended material, condition, quantity, current terms, logistics, evidence and relevant permissions have been checked for the actual proposed scope.

## No-go conditions
Pause the proposed stream if acceptance is unclear, a receiver cannot explain rejection conditions, safe transport/storage is unavailable, a mandatory licence or approval is unresolved, or economics depend on an unconfirmed price. Do not deliver unknown, hazardous, clinical, chemical, battery, electronic or other specialist waste under this guide.
